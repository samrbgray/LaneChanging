2.9 Experiment Design
LC Experiment (tAV as Lane Changer, n = 72 trials)

LCV drives in Subject Lane; merges into Target Lane in front of F1
Independent variables: downstream spacing (DS = 0, 1, 2, 3) and desired vehicle
spacing (DV = 0, 1, 2)
LCV operated in FSD mode; LV in Cruise at 40 mph; F1/F2 in ACC at 40 mph

Responding Experiment (tAV as Responder, n = 80 trials)

LCV merges in front of F1 (operated in FSD); F2 also in FSD
Four driving style configurations: HH, HC, CH, CC (Hurry/Chill combinations)
DS × configuration matrix yields 80 total trials

2.10 Gap Acceptance Protocol

Headway threshold H = 1.5 s (~30 m at 40 mph)
"Easy" gaps: ≥ 1.2 s (10–12 trials attempted)
"Difficult" gaps: ≤ 0.6 s (3–4 trials attempted)
