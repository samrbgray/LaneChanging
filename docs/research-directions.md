# # Purpose: Inspire potential users by surfacing open research questions the dataset can support.
Suggested research directions (draw from slide 38 + team input):

# Behavioral comparison
— Compare LC and responding behavior between tAV modes
(FSD vs. ACC) and human-driven baselines. Quantify differences in gap acceptance,
lateral trajectories, and time-to-lane-change.

# Lane-changing model development
— Revisit or develop new gap acceptance and
trajectory models calibrated specifically for tAV behavior (e.g., revising the
classic Wiedemann or IDM models for FSD).

# Safety assessment
— Use the high-resolution trajectory data to compute
surrogate safety measures (TTC, DRAC, PSD) during LC events across difficulty levels.

# Cooperative driving behavior
— Investigate how FSD and ACC vehicles respond
differently when a vehicle merges in front of them; compare yielding/braking profiles.

# Energy implications
— Link the energy consumption dataset to LC event
trajectories to study how aggressive vs. gentle LC maneuvers affect EV energy use.

# Traffic flow modeling
— Simulate mixed tAV/HV traffic using empirical
behavioral parameters from this dataset to predict macroscopic flow impacts.
