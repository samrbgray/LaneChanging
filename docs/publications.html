Introducing Transitional Autonomous Vehicles: A New Paradigm for Mixed-Traffic Research

Chen, D., Sharma, A., He, Z., Pugh, Z. · arXiv:2603.05716 · 2025

This paper introduces the concept of transitional autonomous vehicles (tAVs) — consumer-level partial-automation systems (SAE Levels 2–3) already deployed at scale on public roads — and argues for their treatment as a distinct behavioral class in traffic modeling and safety research. The paper presents the NC-tALC experimental framework and dataset as a foundation for empirical tAV behavior analysis in mixed-traffic lane-changing scenarios.
