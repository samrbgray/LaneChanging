Open Problems

Research Directions

Questions the NC-tALC dataset is positioned to help answer. We welcome collaboration — reach out at asharm63@ncsu.edu.

01

Behavioral Comparison: FSD vs. ACC vs. Human


Quantify differences in gap acceptance thresholds, lateral trajectory profiles, lane-change duration, and jerk between tAV modes and human-driven baselines. The dataset's controlled experimental design — holding DS and DV constant across modes — is particularly well-suited to isolating driving mode effects.


Gap Acceptance Trajectory Analysis Behavioral Modeling
02

Lane-Changing Model Development


Calibrate or extend established lane-change models (Wiedemann, MOBIL, IDM-LC) using empirical tAV parameters from this dataset. tAVs may exhibit fundamentally different gap acceptance utility functions and lateral acceleration profiles that require new model formulations rather than simple re-calibration.


MOBIL IDM Model Calibration
03

Surrogate Safety Measures During LC Events


Compute time-to-collision (TTC), deceleration to avoid crash (DRAC), and post-encroachment time (PET) at the moment of lane-change initiation and completion across gap difficulty levels. The range of DS conditions in the dataset — from easy to safety-critical gaps — provides a rich basis for safety curve estimation.


TTC DRAC Safety Analysis
04

Cooperative Responding Behavior


Analyze how FSD and ACC vehicles respond when a vehicle cuts in — comparing deceleration onset, magnitude, headway recovery time, and string stability across Hurry/Chill configurations and gap sizes. The Responding experiment's HH/HC/CH/CC matrix isolates driving style effects on platoon-level disturbance propagation.


String Stability Headway Recovery Cooperative Driving
05

Energy Implications of Lane-Change Aggressiveness


Link the energy consumption CAN data to trajectory-derived aggressiveness metrics (peak lateral acceleration, jerk, speed variation) to quantify how tAV lane-change behavior affects EV range. This is especially tractable in the Responding experiment, where deceleration events can be directly attributed to the cut-in maneuver.


EV Energy CAN Bus Eco-Driving
06

Mixed-Traffic Flow Simulation


Use empirically-derived tAV behavioral parameters from this dataset to parameterize microscopic simulation models (SUMO, VISSIM) and study macroscopic traffic flow impacts at varying tAV market penetration rates. How do tAV lane changes affect capacity and throughput on a Major Collector at 10%, 30%, or 50% market share?


SUMO Mixed Traffic Market Penetration
